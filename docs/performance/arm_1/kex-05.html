Independent measurement following the structure of the NICCS ARM self-assessment guide, §3.5 (1)–(7). Not a submitter self-assessment.

  • Algorithm: Loom

  • Parameter sets: LoomKEX-128, LoomKEX-256, LoomKEX-512
