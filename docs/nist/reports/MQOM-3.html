MQOM-3 — Low: correlated-tree standard-MQ bound falls 19–49 bits short

Proof scope

MQOM's security discussion (§5.1, physical pp.73–78) first gives a formal EUF-KO theorem common to both tree variants. It then states that EUF-CMA follows by simulating the signing oracle. For the correlated-tree variant, that step is given by the external FR26 result and assumes partial-guessing one-wayness of MQ (PGOW-MQ). For the one-tree variant, the stated reduction instead relies directly on standard MQ. Thus the specification does discuss EUF-CMA; the gap reported here is confined to the correlated-tree assumption chain.

The specification calls PGOW-MQ weaker than standard MQ because the adversary has a prefix-validation oracle. Section 5.3.2, physical pp.90–91, gives a non-tight reduction from PGOW-MQ to standard MQ. Table 26 (physical p.91) reports these lower bounds:

Field Category Table 26 lower bound Submission target Gap
GF(2) 1 122 143 21
GF(2) 3 188 207 19
GF(2) 5 245 272 27
GF(16) 1 121 143 22
GF(16) 3 171 207 36
GF(16) 5 223 272 49

Every one of the nine correlated-tree sets maps to one of these field/category rows, so the standard-MQ reduction alone certifies a lower bound 19–49 bits below the submission's adopted NIST gate-count target. The specification describes the reduction's loss relative to its full-MQ estimates as 20–65 bits; that is a different comparison from the target gaps tabulated above.

Classification and limits

This is a Low proof gap, not an attack. The calculation does not give an algorithm meeting a Table 26 lower bound. Section 5.3.3 separately analyzes PGOW-MQ directly and argues that its validation oracle is unlikely to provide a significant practical advantage. Those estimates support the parameter rationale but do not turn the non-tight standard-MQ reduction into a proof at the claimed targets.

The nine one-tree sets are outside this report because their stated EUF-CMA reduction relies directly on standard MQ without the PGOW-MQ step. Closing the finding requires a tighter reduction, a sufficiently supported direct PGOW-MQ hardness result at each target, or use of the one-tree construction.

Reproduction

python3 MQOM/reproduce_mqom_3.py

The deterministic standard-library script pins the specification, recomputes the six target gaps from Table 26, and checks parameters.csv to confirm that all nine -ct sets and no -ot sets are in scope. It is a table/model check, not a cryptanalytic attack.

Harness reproduction

From the ngcc-harness checkout, run the pinned source script through the harness-side dispatcher:

python3 nist/reproduce.py MQOM-3
