SNOVA-9 — Informational: Internal signing API binds only the first 64 digest bytes into the vinegar

Defect

The internal signing function absorbs a fixed BYTES_DIGEST (64) bytes of the digest into the vinegar hash (snova_ref.c:882 (SNOVA/submission/SNOVA_Round3/Reference_Implementation/SNOVA_I_K/snova_ref.c#L882)) but len_digest bytes into the target hash (snova_ref.c:501 (SNOVA/submission/SNOVA_Round3/Reference_Implementation/SNOVA_I_K/snova_ref.c#L501)). The optimized and additional trees have the same two lines (source check); the additional tree exports the function as snova_sign_digest_skx. crypto_sign always passes a 64-byte digest.

Evidence

The reproducer signs four 80-byte digests with one key and one caller-supplied salt through the internal API, for I_K, I_S and V_B.

  • Each signature verifies under its own digest and fails under the others.
  • Digests that agree in their first 64 bytes give identical vinegar and different targets, so the difference of the two signatures lies in the secret oil space. The images of that difference under the public S-power maps span 12 of 12 oil dimensions for I_K, 25 of 25 for V_B and 16 of 20 for I_S: one such pair gives the whole oil space for I_K and V_B.
  • Controls: digests that differ inside the first 64 bytes give unrelated vinegar; two messages through crypto_sign with a forced repeated salt show no relation; a temporary copy that absorbs len_digest at line 882 removes the relation.

Classification and limits

Informational / Confirmed / Implementation. The trigger is a repeated salt, which the randomness-reuse rule in the classification policy rates Informational, together with a digest longer than the 64 bytes that Algorithm 14 (p.23) defines, which is outside the specified interface. The specification states that a repeated salt carries no immediate risk (§1.5.3, p.12); that holds for 64-byte digests. The NIST API is not affected. The oil-space test uses the known private key; no key recovery from public data alone was run. With len_digest below 64 the same line reads past the caller's buffer (source reading). The oil-space-difference recovery mechanism is the same as when a repeated salt and identical vinegar are paired with two different targets (as also occurs if a single seed is reused across variants because pkd is omitted from the vinegar hash).

Absorb len_digest bytes in the vinegar hash, or reject other digest lengths.
