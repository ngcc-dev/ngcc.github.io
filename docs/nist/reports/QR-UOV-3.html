QR-UOV-3 — Informational: one repeated signing-RNG output exposes the oil space

QR-UOV selects its vinegar variables, salt, and free variables from external randomness without hedging them with the secret key or message (specification Algorithm 2, §4.2, physical pp.18–19; submitted sign.c:21-46). If two signing calls on different messages receive the same RNG stream, their vinegar vectors are equal. After accounting for canonical sign, the difference of the two signatures is a nonzero vector in the secret oil space.

One such vector is sufficient. For every public quadratic form represented by a symmetric lifted matrix P_i, the polar linear form x -> o^T P_i x of a known oil vector o vanishes on the whole hidden oil space. The common kernel of these public linear forms recovers that space. In the retained runs its rank was V=52 with kernel dimension M=18 for 1q127L3, and V=76 with kernel dimension M=26 for 3q127L3. Expanding those extension-field bases gives the respective 54- and 78-dimensional oil spaces over the base field.

The retained witness resets the shared harness DRBG before two different-message signatures, obtains one oil vector, recovers the common polar kernel, solves the standard known-oil linear system for a fresh message, and checks the forgery with both an independent public model and the submitted library. A pair made with distinct RNG seeds is the negative control:

python3 QR-UOV/reproduce_qruov_3.py
python3 QR-UOV/reproduce_qruov_3.py 3q127L3

The precondition is outside EUF-CMA: the attack needs one exact repetition of the signing RNG stream across two different messages. A correct RNG makes this infeasible. NIST's call §4.B.4 identifies RNG malfunction and nonce reuse as desirable rather than mandatory robustness considerations. Because QR-UOV makes no hedged-signing or reuse-resistance claim, the project rates the result Informational while recording the demonstrated single-repeat forgery. Deriving the signing coins from the secret key, message, fresh randomness, and a parameter-set domain tag would address this failure.

Harness reproduction

From the ngcc-harness checkout, run the pinned source script through the harness-side dispatcher:
