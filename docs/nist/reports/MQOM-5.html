MQOM-5 — Low: Theorem 5.1's concrete EUF-KO bound reaches one 5 to 7.5 bits below 2^lambda oracle queries

Bound

Theorem 5.1 (physical p.74) bounds the EUF-KO advantage by a sum that includes (4N+1) Q_RO Q_IC / 2^(2 lambda), derived in Eq. (15)-(16) (physical p.76) and Eq. (19) (physical p.77). Its hypotheses are Q_RO <= 2^lambda and Q_IC <= 2^(lambda-1). With Q_RO = Q_IC = Q the whole bound reaches one at:

N Q where the bound reaches one Category 1 queries Category 1 gates (2^15 per query)
8192 2^(lambda−7.51) 2^120.49 2^135.49
4096 2^(lambda−7.01) 2^120.99 2^135.99
2048 2^(lambda−6.52) 2^121.48 2^136.48
256 2^(lambda−5.06) 2^122.94 2^137.94

The (4N+1) term contributes 93% to 99% of the bound at those points. All Category 1 boundaries lie below the 2^143 gate comparison. Categories 3 and 5 reach one at about 2^200–2^202 and 2^265–2^267 gates against 2^207 and 2^272.

Classification and limits

Low / Proof gap: a candidate-specific concrete boundary of the submitted theorem inside the category target, with no attack. The queries are public random-oracle and ideal-cipher queries of a key-only adversary, not signing queries. The comparison assumes Q_RO = Q_IC, omits epsilon_mq, and prices a query like one AES call (2^15 gates at Categories 1 and 3, 2^16 at Category 5; the specification gives "2^15 or 2^16" without assigning levels). Without this term the bound still reaches one at 2^(lambda−1.71) queries, about 2^141.3 gates at Category 1, and the hypothesis on Q_IC alone caps the theorem below 2^143; the term adds 3.3 to 5.8 bits of loss. The theorem is EUF-KO only; MQOM-3 covers the separate PGOW-MQ gap.
