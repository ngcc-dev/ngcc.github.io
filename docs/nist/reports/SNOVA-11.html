SNOVA-11 — Low: multigraded divided-power wedge model puts two l=2 SNOVA alternatives below target

Result

Peigen Li and Siyong Tao's Attacking UOV-based Signatures over divided power algebras (posted 2026-09-30, revised 2026-11-14) lifts SNOVA l = 2 instances over F_{16} to multi-UOV systems over F_{256} (o equations of multidegree (2,0), 2o of multidegree (1,1), and o of multidegree (0,2)) and evaluates a multigraded divided-power wedge attack in Table 6 (p. 17) on the three q = 16, l = 2 alternative parameter sets from SNOVA-spec.pdf Table 6 (physical p. 30; extracted in alternative-parameters.csv).

Recomputing Li and Tao's multigraded Hilbert-series formula (Vto <= 0, §3.4; matching Vto in the authors' published Math.magma, commit a292e4c7, which does not itself include a SNOVA cost function) together with our own row-density implementation following §3.4 (3 * rho * H^2 * A(256) with A(256) = 2*8^2 + 8 = 136 bit operations, weighting (2,0), (1,1), (0,2) rows by o, 2o, o and ignoring memory access cost), which reproduces the paper's three Table 6 values, and searching over projection and level tuples (k1, k2, r1, r2, r3) with r_i in {0, 1}, confirms that two of the three l = 2 alternatives fall below their claimed Category 3 and Category 5 targets while q16-I-l2 stays above Category 1:

The nine recommended On-Ramp Round 3 sets (l = 4 and l = 5) are not covered by this l = 2 construction.

Modeling limits and classification

  1. Unproven multigraded Hilbert-series conjecture. As Li and Tao state explicitly in §4 (p. 18), the estimates rest on the unproven multigraded extension of Conjecture 3.6 (§3.4, p. 12). Their small-instance experiments (Table 1, p. 12) cover single-graded UOV systems (n <= 11); no experiment was reported for lifted multigraded SNOVA systems.
  2. Unaccounted memory cost. The cost formula charges only field operations in block Wiedemann (3 * rho * H^2 * 136) and ignores memory. Storing a single vector of H GF(256) elements requires 2^92.64 bits for q16-III-l2 and 2^120.91 bits for q16-V-l2, and storing the sparse Macaulay matrix requires roughly 2^109 and 2^139 bits. Even a modest memory-access charge (log2(H) or mesh penalty) pushes q16-III-l2 back above 207 and raises q16-V-l2 near or above 272.
  3. Alternative-parameter scope. Like SNOVA-7, this affects only the unimplemented Table 6 l = 2 alternative parameter sets, not the nine recommended l = 4 and l = 5 parameter sets.

This is classified as Low / Lead / Design: a candidate-specific, reproducible RAM-gate calculation putting two Table 6 alternatives 2.4 and 9.0 bits below their claimed categories, conditional on an unproven multigraded Hilbert-series conjecture with no SNOVA experiments and omitted exponential memory costs.

Reproduction

python3 SNOVA/reproduce_snova_11.py

The standard-library script pins SNOVA/SNOVA-spec.pdf and SNOVA/alternative-parameters.csv, evaluates the exact multigraded Vto criterion and row-density cost both at the printed Table 6 tuples and across a full projection/level search (r_i in {0, 1}) for all three l = 2 alternatives, and checks r = (0, 0, 0) classical-wedge controls. It is a deterministic cost/model check, not an attack execution.

Harness reproduction

From the ngcc-harness checkout, run the pinned source script through the harness-side dispatcher:

python3 nist/reproduce.py SNOVA-11
