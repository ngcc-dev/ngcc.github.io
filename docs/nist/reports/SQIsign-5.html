SQIsign-5 — Low: pinned ROM bound loses every category guarantee before its target

Theorem 8.1.7 in the pinned specification (physical p. 106) gives SQIsign's EUF-CMA reduction in the random-oracle model. One of its nonnegative additive terms is

(q + 1)(s + 1) / 2^(lambda + 1),

where q bounds random-oracle queries and s bounds signing-oracle queries. At NIST's s=2^64 evaluation baseline, the term reaches one around q=2^(lambda-63): approximately 2^65, 2^129, and 2^193 queries for p324_3, p500_27, and p664_17. These thresholds precede the respective 143-, 207-, and 272-gate category targets.

The separate final min-entropy term printed in the theorem is

(q + s + 1)s / (sqrt(p) 2^lambda).

At q=2^lambda and s=2^64, its base-2 logarithm is approximately -98.8, -188.4, and -270.0. It remains small under the On-Ramp primes and does not repair the earlier statistical-distance loss.

The theorem is stated only in the classical ROM. Remark 8.1.8 obtains a QROM statement only in the Algebraic Isogeny Model. Section 8.1 also says that the underlying complete proof concerns a previous SQIsign version with even-degree responses. The v3 specification adapts that framework to odd-degree responses but retains the displayed loss.

Why the later Round-2 bound does not narrow this finding

Mamah and Jao's security-proof note improves the Round-2 min-entropy analysis from O(1/sqrt(p)) to O(1/p). Their Theorem 3 uses the commitment-degree condition Nmix > p^2. Under that condition its two statistical terms are

(q+1) 2 sqrt(s+1) / sqrt(p) + 16 (q+s+1)s / p.

SQIsign v3 does not meet the premise. Table 9 (physical p. 92) fixes

Dmix = next_prime(p * 2^(2*lambda)).

Ignoring the negligible next-prime increment, this is below p^2 by 69.58, 120.75, and 156.09 bits for the three On-Ramp sets. The v3 specification accordingly cites the Mamah--Jao result only while saying tighter reductions “may be possible” and leaves that work for the future immediately before Theorem 8.1.7.

Directly substituting the v3 primes into the later formula gives hypothetical thresholds near 2^129.79, 2^219.38, and 2^301.04 random-oracle queries, but those numbers do not establish a v3 theorem. The previous report used them to say that Levels III and V were covered. That narrowing was unsupported and has been removed. The applicable pinned guarantee remains loose for all three sets.

This remains Low / Proof gap. Neither calculation gives an attack: no signature is forged, no key is recovered, and the proof term does not estimate SQIsign's actual forgery advantage. A v3 proof satisfying the later theorem's premises, a new tight analysis for the v3 commitment distribution, or an accepted per-key query limit would close the gap.
