SQIsign-6 — Low: Hint-distinguishing premise of Theorem 8.1.7 is false as printed

Premise

Theorem 8.1.7 (physical p.106) bounds forgery by terms that include the advantage against Problem 8.1.5, distinguishing hints from H^sim and H^unif (Fig. 4, physical p.105). Each hint reveals the degree d of the response isogeny.

  • H^sim line 4 samples phi_2 uniformly among all isogenies of odd degree below D_rsp, with no cyclicity condition, matching the signer, which samples lattice elements with no primitivity test (Algorithm 4.83, physical p.52). The degree law is then proportional to sigma(n), the number of subgroups of order n.
  • H^unif line 2 draws the degree with weight prod (p_i + 1) p_i^(e_i − 1), which is psi(n), the number of cyclic subgroups, and line 3 then samples a possibly non-cyclic isogeny of that degree.

sigma(n) = psi(n) only for squarefree n; for example sigma(9) = 13 and psi(9) = 12.

Evidence

The reproducer confirms these lines on their pages of the pinned specification and computes the two degree laws over odd n:

  • Total variation 0.01158; Pr[9 | d] is 0.1440 under H^sim and 0.1333 under H^unif (limits 35/243 and 2/15).
  • Counting hints with 9 | d distinguishes the laws at 3 sigma with about 2^13.1 hints and at 8 sigma with about 2^16.0; a Hellinger bound gives distinguishing advantage at least 1/2 from 5,305 hints. The theorem's bound therefore exceeds one far below NIST's 2^64 signing queries.
  • On toy supersingular graphs (p = 191, 383) the exact H^sim degree law is within 2·10^-5 of the sigma law and 0.0116 from the printed psi law.
  • Controls: the laws agree on squarefree degrees, and a cyclic-only H^sim would match psi.

Classification and limits

Low / Proof gap: a candidate-specific false premise with a concrete boundary inside NIST's budget. No forgery follows, since real signatures follow H^sim. The exact distance at the submitted primes and D_rsp was not computed; only the degree model, its limit and toy graphs. Only the degree of phi_2 is compared. If H^sim was meant to draw cyclic isogenies only, the gap moves to Lemma 8.1.3, where the simulator would no longer match the signer. Weighting the H^unif degree by sigma(n) would repair the premise. SQIsign-5 is the separate statistical loss of the same theorem.
