UOV-4 — Low: truncated-ring estimates leave almost no uov-V RAM margin

Result

Re-evaluating the specification's §4.6 truncated-polynomial-ring and Furue–Ikematsu intersection formulas gives the following minimum RAM-gate costs:

Parameter set Recomputed cost Claimed reference Margin
uov-Ip 144.75 143 +1.75
uov-Is 158.55 143 +15.55
uov-III 213.12 207 +6.12
uov-V 272.05 272 +0.05

The uov-V result is effectively on its claimed boundary under the submission's primary RAM-gate model; uov-Ip has less than two bits of margin. Table 5 reports floor(log2(cost)): 144, 158, 213 and 272, rather than values rounded to the nearest integer. Flooring the recomputed values gives exactly those four entries. The calculation does not place a On-Ramp Round 3 parameter set below its claim.

The post-submission Schur–Macaulay estimator was separately reviewed with the authors' full shape search. It gives about 143.9, 158.1, 216.6 and 280.7 for Ip, Is, III and V, respectively, so it also does not lower a On-Ramp Round 3 set below its target. An earlier 271.7 figure for uov-V came from a 0.32-bit row-counting convention applied to plain wedge, rather than the new Schur attack.

Reproduction and interpretation

python3 UOV/reproduce_uov_4.py

The script implements the specification formulas with exact integer power series and prints every On-Ramp Round 3 optimum. It is a deterministic cost/model check, not a key-recovery run. The specification also reports substantial MEM-2D additions for these attacks, including 48 bits for uov-V, and warns that the underlying semi-regularity heuristics have counterexamples.

This report records a Low Lead because the candidate-specific reproduced calculation leaves only 0.05 bit of RAM-model margin for uov-V and 1.75 bits for uov-Ip, while no current estimate establishes an attack below target under a complete memory-aware model. The rating follows the methodology's Low / Lead rule for a reproducible candidate-specific primary-model estimate with less than two bits of margin and an unresolved modeling choice that could erase it. Parameter changes would be warranted only after that missing step is resolved.
