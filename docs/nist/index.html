NIST additional digital signatures: independent security reports

This is an independent review of the nine submissions listed by NIST for the third round of its additional post-quantum signature process. It applies the NIST-specific classification policy to the call's security requirements and each submission's own claims. Ratings are project assessments, not NIST findings.

The official NIST call for proposals and Round 3 candidate page define the evaluation baseline and candidate set. Each candidate row links directly to the specification PDF and submission ZIP on csrc.nist.gov. Package rounds and candidate status come from the public harness registry.

Severity is color-coded: Critical High Medium Low Info. Evidence status is separate: Confirmed, Probable, Lead, and Proof gap.
